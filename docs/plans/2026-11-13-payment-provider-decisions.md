# Payment provider decisions and source record

13 November 2026. Public primary-document research and proposed design choices; no account, credentials, transaction, payout or integration was operated. Recheck the exact legal terms, account capabilities and API versions before activation. The operator's country/entity is unknown. No machine locale is treated as merchant eligibility. [PAYMENTS.md](../PAYMENTS.md) specifies the flows, authorities and acceptance; E3 governs creators and E4 governs Studio service sales independently.

## Choices and tradeoffs

**Studio:** use Stripe Checkout/Billing/customer portal as the reference implementation, conditional on E4. It offers a clear subscription/one-time purchase path and lets our server enforce bounded prepaid consumption. The operator remains responsible for ordinary merchant tax, refunds, disputes and service operations; this is the cost of that control. If those duties are impractical, evaluate Paddle for the complete disclosed offering and approve a separate MoR decision before implementation. Do not build both for launch or silently change processor. Neither a Stripe account nor a software license proves approval to aggregate seller funds or resell AI-provider subscriptions.

**MoR alternatives:** Paddle's AI documentation describes subscriptions and credit packs, but its AUP also covers stored value, marketplaces, mass marketing, phone services and content generation including images/videos and impersonation. Our broader module catalog makes product review material. Do not conceal modules or treat a small software SKU as approval of the full business. Lemon Squeezy supports digital goods but prohibits marketplaces selling others' products, services, PLR/MRR and certain business-opportunity products. A legitimate original Studio tool still requires review of its actual claims, functions and pack model. Reduced covered transaction-tax administration does not eliminate our income tax, product, privacy, support or contractual duties.

**Creator first sale:** propose each creator's own approved Lemon Squeezy store for original downloadable artifacts, provider checkout/file fulfillment, and manual setup where writes are unavailable. Its public API supports checkout creation and retrieval of products/files; the inspected API navigation does not expose product creation or file upload. Record a manual setup/read-back, never invent an API. This trades some setup work for provider-held delivery while the desktop is asleep. Do not funnel all creators through our store or substitute an affiliate program for joint settlement. Existing approved destinations remain selectable through WP27's actual capability contract.

**Other creator routes:** ordinary Stripe Checkout can collect a creator's own payments but needs an independently accepted always-on fulfillment service; payment processing alone does not host a document/course. Whop remains a course/membership/delivery candidate requiring actual account/API tests, tax-mode selection, country/payout eligibility and product review. Full learning/store controls remain WP46. Connect is a conditional platform path, initially direct charges for a single creator if approved; destination/separate-transfer flows and automated partner splits remain disabled until funds-flow/liability/country/reversal operations are accepted. No new work package or capability is needed merely to add missing safety/lifecycle criteria.

**Current Stripe MoR distinction:** Stripe also offers Managed Payments for eligible direct digital sales. It is a distinct reviewed product, not a property inherited by standard Checkout/Billing. The inspected documentation excludes Connect/platform integration and has product/location/integration limits. Keep it as a future explicit E4 comparison if desired; no assumption that it solves creator marketplace payments or professional-service invoicing.

**Auth/service dependency:** payment contracts accept authenticated actor/organization/role, not a mandatory hosted-auth vendor. The [service decision](../SERVICES.md) now prefers Clerk with official Convex integration while keeping Convex as hosted authority/storage/coordination and excluding duplicate Clerk Billing authority. Local operation, creator-owned external merchant checkout and provider-held delivery must not become dependent on a paid Studio plan, a particular auth vendor or a general connector broker. An always-on receiver/worker is necessary only for the specific accepted hosted/event/fulfillment capability. The wider hosting/auth/connectors decision is maintained separately.

## Official evidence consulted

The statements below are bounded summaries of current official pages, not copied legal terms or approval evidence. Every link was retrieved or inspected during this task on 13 November 2026. Country lists, fees and preview status change; record the applicable version/account evidence at E3/E4 rather than freezing universal eligibility in the product.

| ID | Primary source | What it supports / design consequence |

| S01 | [Stripe global availability](https://stripe.com/global) | Merchant onboarding availability differs from buyer reach. Extended-network labels do not establish identical Stripe product/Connect eligibility. |
| S02 | [Stripe restricted businesses](https://stripe.com/legal/restricted-businesses) | Stored-value/credits, content platforms and third-party payment aggregation have specific restrictions or review; approval is service-specific and revocable. Record actual business review. |

| S28 | [Whop sandbox](https://docs.whop.com/developer/guides/sandbox.md) | Separate sandbox/production accounts, hosts, keys and webhooks; SDK production default and similar key appearance make explicit mode binding necessary. No account was created or tested. |
