# First useful result and bounded service flows

Design decision, 13 November 2026. Additive, design-only requirements under the owner's authorized scope. Read with [PRODUCT](../PRODUCT.md), [ROADMAP](../ROADMAP.md) and the [work-package contracts](../WORK-PACKAGES.md). Nothing here is implemented or demonstrated by this repository.

## Intent and approach

A novice should reach one useful, reviewable result without writing prompts or understanding the full module catalog. An experienced user should be able to repeat that job, inspect its inputs and costs, edit the result elsewhere, and prepare a precise handoff. Preserve the existing desktop architecture, shared business graph, all registered modules and exact provider/account/model policy.

Compose the existing brief, recommendations, artifacts, harness and workflows. A separate persona catalog would duplicate these contracts; a mandatory CRM-first funnel would delay a useful product export. The selected approach adds acceptance within existing work-package IDs. Three new requirement rows describe bounded service jobs, proof bundles and split scenarios; none needs a new engine, persistent persona or work package. One later Dot retains its existing gates.

The design was informed by supplied research summaries of selected presentation frames and closing machine-transcribed speech. That is partial coverage, not a full watch, recovered private prompts, tested outputs, verified leads, delivery receipts or measured costs/income. The gaps below are missing integration specifications in our plan, not proof of missed source evidence or claims that another product lacks these behaviors. These are original product requirements; subsequent research can refine the evidence separately.

## The first useful result

At M1, group existing brief fields into five short, editable prompts: desired outcome; knowledge/skills and permitted material; reachable audience or people the user can help; available time and budget; prior attempts and constraints. Allow unknowns and skip nonessential detail. Rights, language and budget remain existing fields, not a second onboarding record.

Return one recommended route and one backup, with fit rationale, evidence gaps, maximum authorized cost/work, first small deliverable and next checkpoint. A backup is an alternative project route, never permission to swap a selected model. If evidence is insufficient, recommend a bounded investigation and state the gap. A later service/creator route may be shown as planned while an eligible document sample remains available. Reuse saved answers; changes version the brief and stale dependent advice. No income promise or automatic reminder/wake schedule.

An optional editable 30-day execution plan groups weekly checkpoints and first-week actions around the selected route, current capability gates, time/budget assumptions and a user-adjustable activity target. At M1 it is revisioned recommendation data with manual progress and user-requested check-ins, not the sellable M2 action-plan product. It requires no CRM, calendar account or Dot. Turning eligible steps into M3 tasks/campaigns is an explicit later choice; advice never starts reminders, external actions or persistent wakes. Activity targets are hypotheses, not promised replies or income.

The M2 journey is: brief and decision → short original guide/workbook sample → editorial review → editable artifact and local export → reopen and next decision. It ends usefully without a contact, creator, campaign, mailbox, merchant or cloud account. Measure setup, research/generation, human review, export and successful reopening separately, plus interventions/abandonment, in the existing novice pilot. Record observations, not a promised completion time. All five document families remain required.

## Reusable jobs and service delivery

A job card is the readable view of an existing definition/run plan: purpose; required inputs and reused context revisions; example output; exact requested provider/account/model/options and host; effective identity or unverified state; required versus granted tools; budget and cost unknowns; approval stops; acceptance criteria; and the next handoff. Show only eligible actions. M0C supplies the card/inspector contract; M1/M2 use it for their shipped jobs. Search, favorites and multi-job composition remain WP21/M3. Saving a card never saves credentials or a reusable action approval. Reruns preserve manual edits and revalidate evidence, model, rights, grants and budget.

Keep two outcomes distinct:

- A **product pack** is a reusable guide, workbook, template kit, curriculum or action plan for a buyer segment (M2).
- A **service job** is work for one identified client/contact (M3): permitted input revisions and allowed uses, requested deliverable and format, factual/brand constraints, scope exclusions, revision allowance, completion checks and handoff method. Label terms proposed until an attributed agreement is recorded. Export-ready, handed off and client-accepted are different states; local export proves none of the latter two.

Use the existing workflow/context/artifact records for a `ServiceJob` view, not a new CRM or invoice system. Original acceptance example: a local repair business supplies an authorized service sheet and logo for one editable ad brief/copy sample, with one revision round and a manual export handoff. Recheck factual claims and mark the sample illustrative. A scope change creates a new revision for review. Never infer permission from a public listing or fabricate a client order. Generated image variants and finished video require separately accepted M4A capabilities; a script/storyboard remains a script/storyboard. Client portal, e-sign and invoice operations remain M8A.

## Proof bundle before outreach

WP17/M2 exports the reviewed sample with editable source, a readable rendition and a rights-safe provenance/limitations manifest. No recipient is required. WP22/M3 composes that existing sample into a `ProofBundle` with the exact artifact revision/hash, proposed recipient/contact revision, channel and exact unsent message version. Include the observed reason for fit, source date, contact-verification status, unresolved assumptions and a concrete next-step request. Fit is a hypothesis; a public address is not verified deliverability or buying intent.

Separate the recipient-safe sample from private research, client inputs, cost details and internal notes. Review the disclosure before copy/export. Changing the recipient, sample or message invalidates bundle review. Copy/export is a complete M3 result labeled **unsent**; it cannot upload a remote draft, send or publish. M4B rechecks current replies, suppression, sender readiness and exact payload approval before dispatch. A draft, tool success string or local handoff note is never a sent/delivery receipt. Publication uses M4C. Dot correspondence is not a prospecting shortcut.

## Creator split scenarios and visible costs

WP20/M3 adds a built-in deterministic proposal worksheet under the existing proposal capability, not generated executable software or merchant accounting. Snapshot currency, price, time window, reachable exposure and its source/assumption, conversion assumptions including **zero**, refund quantity/value, fixed and variable fees, fee treatment on refunds, cost responsibility and proposed split basis/percentages. Followers/views do not automatically equal reachable buyers. Unknown exposure yields no forecast; user-entered exposure yields an explicitly hypothetical scenario.

Show gross proceeds, refunds, fees, pre-split net, each proposed share and each party's costs/exposure separately. Define whether costs are deducted before splitting or borne after; never deduct them twice. Use exact minor currency units, declared rounding and a reconciliation remainder; validate percentages and refund bounds. Negative net remains a visible loss with loss allocation unresolved until agreed, not a fabricated negative payout. Zero sales may still incur fixed costs. Unknown fees/refunds/costs keep affected totals unknown or conditional. Provide sensitivity cases, not a probability or earnings guarantee. Export the assumptions with the proposal; changing them creates a new revision. Agreements, actual sales, refunds and payouts retain their later external gates.

Every run view separates already-owned access and recurring subscriptions from incremental job estimates, reserved amounts, provider-reported actual charges, failed/retried work and unknowns. Record currency, period, tool route and receipt/quote time; website allowances do not imply API/automated-route entitlement. Show measured human setup/review time separately from optional valuation assumptions. Incomplete actuals are a known subtotal plus unknown components, never an all-in total or zero. Parent run views declare whether they include child work; shared operation/attempt and receipt identities prevent counting nested charges twice. Unresolved attribution stays unknown. At M2, open/edit an exported document outside the app and import/restore a portable project bundle preserving supported versions, edits, hashes and allowed evidence/rights metadata. External edits become a new revision through supported import or explicit manual reconciliation; do not promise lossless arbitrary-format round trips.
