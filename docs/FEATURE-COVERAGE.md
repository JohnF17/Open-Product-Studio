Research baseline: 12 November 2026; original flow requirements added 13 November 2026. This register has **304 traceable rows**: 49 bundle-advertised core, 49 demo-visible, 148 wider vendor ecosystem, 22 human/community/promotion and 36 original product requirements. The original 286-row census is preserved; 15 owner-requested harness/council/Dot requirements and three original service/proof-bundle/scenario requirements extend it. Rows overlap and are not unique internal agents or independent engineering tickets.
**Evidence:** 231 advertised/documented, 37 recorded and 36 requirements-only. Zero paid features were independently operated/tested. A published/composited promotional interface is advertising evidence; a recorded demonstration establishes only its displayed behavior. Offer versions remain separate. No current purchase entitlement or outcome is validated.
The [canonical roadmap](ROADMAP.md) has **66 work packages and 112 owned capabilities**. The later offline Day Trader replay capability is an original Studio requirement outside the historic feature census. ## Decisions after the census

- [Service architecture](SERVICES.md) and [project experience](PROJECT-EXPERIENCE.md) refine existing rows, preserving the complete inventory and original observations. Clerk/Convex hosted identity, immutable project/account context, conditional creator/email adapters, transfer custody, authenticated exit, deletion-aware restore, first-activation operations, qualification expiry and output accessibility are design requirements, not newly observed vendor behavior. That review covered the original 66 packages, 111 capabilities and 304 rows. Current implementation evidence is recorded per work package.

- The [payment design](PAYMENTS.md) refines existing OPS-017 and merchant checkout/refund/tax/payout acceptance. Separate payer organizations, subscriptions/seats, prepaid service units, creator merchants and buyer access; preserve all 304 rows, 111 capabilities and 66 packages. No provider approval or working payment integration is claimed.

- The [first-result/service design](plans/2026-11-13-first-result-and-service-flows.md) distinguishes existing coverage, refined composition and new bounded contracts. Diagnostic/cards/portability/cost criteria refine existing rows; OPS-019/020/021 add service jobs, exact unsent proof bundles and creator split scenarios within existing packages. M2 export stays independent of M3. These are original requirements, not new claims of observed vendor behavior.
- Upsell copy is explicit in WP22/M3, tied to a configured additional offer; unconfigured checkout remains a draft.
- M0C adds the tested harness before AI-dependent M1 actions; M3C adds councils. M9A-D introduces a single proactive personal Dot, mail/browser/Reach, verified phone, then narrowly scoped standing actions.
- Core functions remain M0A-M6C. Wider lead/inbox/sender, learning/store and merchant operations have explicit M7A-M7D extension gates; client portal, booking, communications and integration automation have M8A-M8D gates. These are coherent optional modules, not unfinished core features hidden behind payment.
- Every licensed data, sender, API, buyer-access and merchant capability requires account-specific supported scopes. Import/export/manual handoff stays useful when a provider API is unavailable.
- Human support, private courses, competitions, certificates, prizes and vendor seat entitlements remain named external responsibilities. Original tutorials/feedback templates cannot be advertised as those services.
- Alchemy of Self behavior and the three unexpanded Synthesise formats remain unknown. The private over-fifty-agent catalog/prompt chains and backend model/training data are not known. No private parity claim is made.
## Significant source corrections
The official [ListKit partner-seat guide](https://docs.listkit.io/en/articles/17601368-i-m-a-monetise-or-client-ascension-member-what-s-included-and-how-do-i-get-help) describes limited account-specific domains/inboxes, credits and daily caps. Its managed domains are sending-only and vendor-owned; transfers are discretionary. A full Bundle and its API/data capacity cannot be inferred from a partner-seat valuation. Our connector must discover actual entitlement and ownership.
Current [ListKit analytics guidance](https://docs.listkit.io/en/articles/17116688-how-do-i-read-my-results-in-analytics-and-why-are-there-no-open-rates) explicitly excludes open/click tracking. Other connected channels may have those metrics, but this connector cannot invent them. Flozy pages mark some calls/screensharing as coming soon, and generic integration language is not a tested adapter.
The current offer, closed/historic offers, standalone tools and wider third-party product documentation describe different entitlements. The catalog preserves the offerVariant rather than treating their union as one purchased bundle. Marketing duration, source counts, revenue examples and unlimited-credit wording are not performance/quality/earnings evidence.
