# Affiliate Storefront

Research checked 19 November 2026 against public first-party sources.

## Working local builder

Open Projects → Affiliate Storefront. Discover records candidate names, source links, buyer fit and dated evidence privately. Compare pins the products to include. On Links, write fresh customer-facing copy, enter the merchant-issued destination and add a disclosure. Design offers an editorial page and a catalog grid. Review shows the exact public HTML. Export saves the approved version, and the project resumes on its last sheet. Changing public copy, cards or layout requires another review. Choosing a different local file format preserves the approved content.

## Product case

A creator making a desk-setup clip wants to help viewers choose among three stands and send interested viewers to the right seller. In Studio, they build one shortlist, explain who each option suits, prepare a disclosed comparison page and reuse the same reviewed claims in a clip. The merchant handles checkout, fulfillment and returns. A creator selling their own inventory needs a separate merchant flow with its own account, order and payment evidence. ## Planned expansion of the local workflow

| Step | What the creator does |
| --- | --- |
| **Discover** | Start with an audience and buying criteria, then browse manually or paste permitted listing or social references. Record merchant, product/variant ID, market, source URL, observation date, and permitted uses for each claim and asset. A social URL can remain a note. Use original descriptions and owned or expressly licensed imagery. Keep unsupported claims out of the copy. |
| **Compare** | Compare two or more products side by side by use case, variant, condition, specs, seller, availability, price/currency, shipping and return constraints where evidenced. Show the observation date, distinguish seller claims from firsthand tests, and leave missing values unknown. Keep commission terms in the operator view and base recommendations on buyer fit. Suppress live-price claims when freshness or program terms cannot be met. |
| **Links** | Keep the source URL and the program-issued tracked link or native product tag as distinct fields. Record the intended market, channel, seller, item, affiliate account alias and allowed placement. Choose an ordinary product reference or omit the CTA when the program or destination remains unqualified. |
| **Design** | Use a comparison grid with a clear audience-fit note per product, or a single-product editorial page with one primary CTA. For a social companion, use owned footage, captions and a short comparison cue. Keep the disclosure adjacent to the purchasing action and visible in the video where applicable. |
| **Review and export** | Review every claim, asset permission, disclosure, link and intended destination on the same saved revision. Preview at mobile and desktop widths, with keyboard and reduced motion. Export a local static page, a Shopify placement brief, or a handoff for the user's chosen stack. Carry editable card data and private evidence separately from public page files. |

### Destination and rights checks

- Validate HTTPS, expected seller/product or native tag, marketplace and locale, correct variant, approved account and placement, and intact program tracking. For provider-issued short links, verify the final supported destination in the owner's approved preview or test route on mobile and desktop. Flag stale, changed, region-redirected, unavailable or ambiguous destinations. A later automated checker must restrict redirect hops to qualifying public hosts, run without account cookies and avoid counting validation as shopper traffic. Avoid link rewriting, cookie injection, invisible redirects and synthetic clicks to test commission.
- Disclose near the relevant links in plain language such as “I may earn a commission if you buy through these links.” Use an understandable in-video disclosure as well as the caption where applicable. Use required native disclosure tools, including Meta's paid partnership label when applicable. Apply program-specific wording and the audience's local rules. [FTC influencer guidance (US)](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers)
- Check rights per asset and derivative, including photos, reviews, fonts, code, audio and footage. Link to references where reuse rights are unknown. A public listing, a source link and a working downloader establish different things. Any later authorized catalog connector gets its own field, caching, display and reuse rules. Publishing gets separate approval bound to revision, account, product tags, settings and destination. Reconcile an ambiguous submit by its original operation ID before retrying.
- Before enabling any live action, the program/account owner records their residence and legal entity market, audience and shipping market, account type and standing, approved channel, app scopes, product category, payout currency, tax/KYC status, reporting access, fees and attribution/return rules. Recheck at dispatch. The machine's timezone does not establish enrollment or eligibility for Kenya or any other market.

**Privacy and cost.** Keep drafts, evidence and reports local by default. Store only needed identifiers and aggregate outcomes. Keep tokens in the secure account store and exclude buyer PII, private notes and raw reports from public export. Set retention and deletion for cached catalog data, previews and reports. Before any external job, show what data leaves the device, the account or service, usage ceiling, applicable fees and retry cost. Hosting, domains, Shopify plans/themes/apps, program requirements and paid design/model tools are separate cost decisions.

**Reconciliation.** Keep observed outbound clicks, program-attributed referrals/orders, pending estimates, approved commissions, reversals and paid cash distinct. Import authorized statements keyed by program/account, market, transaction or line ID, report revision and currency. Preserve source dates and attribution windows, dedupe repeat imports and apply refunds, cancellations, partial returns and chargebacks as linked adjustments. Match net approved amounts, fees/withholding and actual payout deposits within the same settlement period and currency. Leave missing reports or unmatched amounts unknown. For a synthetic check, 100 clicks and 3 referrals can yield 2 approvals, one later reversed, and one commission ultimately paid. Studio observations alone cannot establish earnings.

**Platform research limits.** TikTok Shop Partner Center search and authorization descriptions came from public indexing because the direct page returned a JavaScript shell. The Meta announcements describe selected regions or partners. Account eligibility, authenticated catalog behavior, live publication and commissions remain unverified. The local evidence above covers Studio's own files and renderer.
