# Payments, service entitlements and creator commerce

Design only, 13 November 2026. Nothing here connects an account or authorizes a charge. Read the [provider decision and official sources](plans/2026-11-13-payment-provider-decisions.md), [platform boundaries](PLATFORM-BOUNDARIES.md), [security model](SECURITY-MODEL.md) and [work-package contracts](WORK-PACKAGES.md). Provider choices below are proposals conditional on account, product, country and operational acceptance, not claims of eligibility or legal guarantees.

## Decisions and scope

Keep useful open-source local editing, supported personal CLI/API/local-model connections, tutorials and installed exports independent of Studio sign-in, subscriptions and donations. Personal provider subscriptions remain the user's own access under supported terms. Hosted jobs use licensed commercial APIs; no resale of CLI subscriptions, pooled consumer OAuth, quota evasion or unapproved proxy. Donations use separately verified optional links (E5), never service credits or paid permissions.

Propose Stripe Checkout/Billing and its customer portal as the reference implementation for Studio's own recurring service subscriptions and one-time prepaid service packs (WP35). This provides a concrete implementation contract while E4 still blocks live sales until the Studio operator's legal entity, supported country, product approval, tax duties and economics are verified. Ordinary Stripe processing leaves us the merchant; Stripe Tax does not by itself transfer those duties. Paddle is a separately approved MoR alternative if reducing covered transaction-tax operations outweighs fees and product restrictions. Its current policy restricts/prohibits parts of this Studio's broader media/marketing/phone scope: disclose the complete offering and obtain explicit approval, rather than assume AI-SaaS marketing approves every module. No automatic processor failover or duplicate customer subscription.

For a first creator download, propose an independently approved Lemon Squeezy store selling that creator's original digital product, with provider checkout/file delivery and manual product/file setup where its public API lacks writes. This is not a Studio master store selling other creators' products. A creator may instead use an approved existing processor/destination; preserve truthful export/handoff if no adapter qualifies. Courses/memberships need a separately qualified destination (Whop is a candidate), not a claim that a checkout implements an LMS. Keep application fees and automated partner splits off in the initial commerce path. Stripe Connect is a conditional platform expansion within WP27/WP47, requiring a reviewed funds-flow decision and account-specific evidence before activation.

These extend existing contracts, not the registry: retain 66 work packages, 111 capabilities and all 304 coverage rows. Subscription/seat entitlements fit `cap.connections.credits`/WP35's existing service-billing boundary. Seat eligibility never activates unshipped collaboration; M6C/WP42 remains required. Provider-specific implementation can be reviewed in separate changes under those packages without adding a new custody or wallet service.

## Authorities and identities

| Concept | Authority and binding | Explicit separation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Personal model access | User's provider account on the chosen host | No Studio balance or subscription required for local use; unknown external cost stays unknown |
| Studio service subscription | BillingOrganization, payer legal entity, provider customer/subscription, immutable environment and plan version | Optional storage/service/seat access, separate from inference units and local ownership |
| Hosted service units | Trusted Studio ledger for the paying BillingOrganization | Nontransferable consumption of our named services, never money, third-party checkout tender or withdrawable earnings |
| Creator sale | MerchantConnection for creator legal entity, provider account/store, mode, seller role and destination | Buyer payment, seller balance and tax responsibilities do not belong to the Studio usage ledger |
| Buyer delivery | Provider buyer/order identity plus exact offer/artifact access policy | Creator's Studio seat, payer identity and AI account do not authenticate a buyer |
| Partner compensation | External provider's supported settlement contract, or separately recorded human accounting | Local proposal percentages, affiliate referrals, transfers and bank payouts are different records |

An authenticated person may belong to several payer organizations and control several creator merchants. The active project is navigation context, never a payment principal. `BillingOrganizationId`, `BusinessProjectId`, `MerchantConnectionId` and buyer identity are distinct. Show payer, merchant/destination and test/live mode on each relevant screen. A project stores an explicit authorized billing binding for hosted work and an explicit merchant binding for its offer; neither is inferred from the most recently opened project, email address or default account. Switching projects cannot retarget an open checkout, portal session, reservation, invoice, refund or publication.

Resolve actor/membership/role on the server. Bind action digest, organization/merchant, provider account, environment, project/offer revision, currency and amount ceiling at preparation; recheck at dispatch. A changed binding invalidates the preview. Reject cross-organization customer/session/order IDs and stale member/seat grants. Hosted authorization contracts remain vendor-neutral: the [service decision](SERVICES.md) prefers Clerk through the official Convex integration, with Convex as hosted business authority and private storage/coordination. Clerk Billing is not enabled alongside the direct billing adapter. A sign-in vendor or connected-app broker cannot grant billing or merchant authority.

## Studio customer flow

1. **Choose local or hosted.** Local remains immediately useful. Hosted requires an explicit account and payer organization, eligible supported service and disclosure of the content destinations. Select/create the organization and billing legal entity with owner/billing-admin authority. Organization membership is not permission to buy, change seats or refund.
2. **Check readiness.** Show service availability, payer role, seller-country/entity approval, supported buyer location/payment method, configured currencies, tax/invoice details and terms version. Never infer business country from timezone, IP or machine locale. Obtain required identity/bank/tax verification through the payment provider; retain approval/status references rather than raw identity documents. Unverified/ineligible means local use continues and hosted checkout is unavailable. Test mode is visibly distinct and cannot buy production access.
3. **Choose what to buy.** Separate a recurring subscription (defined storage/service/seat limits) from a one-time inference/service pack. Paid collaboration is offered only after WP42 is accepted. Initial proposal: no bundled recurring inference allowance, no free trial, no auto-recharge, no automatic overage and no cross-currency balance conversion. Additional models are exact selections with their own rates, never an unlimited promise. Do not require a subscription solely to spend a supported prepaid pack.
4. **Review checkout.** Server allowlists immutable product/price IDs, currency, quantity and environment. Show seller/MoR name, payer, amount, tax-inclusive/exclusive treatment, final taxes/total at provider checkout, recurring interval/renewal, seat quantity, service units and published rate conversion, refund/expiry terms, invoice/receipt and support routes. Currency uses its declared exponent, not always two decimals; service units use a separate exact integer scale. Expired quotes require renewed review. Use hosted browser checkout with allowlisted return URLs; desktop deep links only resume status lookup. No card data or secret API keys enter the renderer.
5. **Wait for authoritative payment.** `created -> requires-action/processing -> paid | failed | expired`, with separate fulfillment and refund/dispute state. Closing checkout is not proof of cancellation or failure. Returning successfully is not proof of payment. Authenticated server status plus verified provider events/history drive access and pack grants, exactly once per purchase/invoice line. Delayed methods stay pending. A spoofed return URL or local success flag never charges, mints units or activates a seat.
6. **Use a bounded hosted job.** Select project, billed organization, host and exact model/options. Show quote ceiling, service-unit estimate, spendable/reserved units and known/unknown costs. Confirm content disclosure and job grant. The server atomically reserves units before admission, rechecks role/entitlement/grant/ownership epoch immediately before dispatch, and settles attributable usage once. Insufficient units stop admission without changing the model. Local execution remains a separate route with the user's own provider costs.
7. **Cancel work.** Distinguish queue cancellation, cancel requested, provider stopped, completion and unknown outcome. Release unused units after evidence; consumed provider work may still cost. A cancellation button is not a refund receipt. Show partial artifacts, usage receipts and unresolved reservations. A lost response does not authorize another paid attempt.
8. **Manage service access.** An authenticated payer-scoped portal supports payment-method updates, invoice/receipt downloads and cancellation. Proposed subscription cancellation takes effect at the paid-through date; show it and disable renewal. Do not erase prepaid packs on subscription cancellation. No implicit upgrade proration or extra seat invoice: prepare exact provider preview and approval; default downgrades/seat reductions at renewal, preserve read/export and block new over-cap actions. Active, incomplete, trialing if later enabled, past-due, canceled, paused and unpaid map to an explicit access policy. Proposed first release has no unpaid spending grace; paid access expires at its verified paid-through boundary unless an explicit bounded trial/policy applies. Buying seats does not invite users or change roles; seat assignment checks paid capacity and current membership atomically.
9. **Request a refund or resolve a dispute.** Show original purchase, remaining unreserved units, consumed/reserved amounts, applicable tax/terms and provider case status. Proposed commercial policy: unused purchased units do not expire initially and may be requested for refund to the original payment method, subject to approved provider terms and mandatory rights. Promotional units are separate, disclosed, nonrefundable and may expire. Freeze the refundable units atomically before a refund, reconcile pending jobs and provider tax/fee adjustments, then remove units only with a confirmed correction; pending/failed refunds stay visible and cannot race with spend. Consumed-service/duplicate-charge/dispute cases go to named support review, not an invented blanket denial. No wallet withdrawal or payment to another user.
10. **Leave or delete.** Export projects and finance receipts before cancellation/deletion without buying a renewed plan; offer an authenticated hosted export/read-only grace window (proposed 30 days, finance/privacy review required) and local portable continuation. Account deletion stops future jobs, renewals where controllable and sessions, inventories provider-held subscriptions/refunds/disputes, minimizes contact data and schedules content deletion. Disclosed accounting/dispute/anti-replay records and backup expiry can outlast profile deletion. Membership removal or a project copy never transfers packs, billing credentials or merchant authority.

## Ledger, payment events and recovery

Use a service consumption ledger, not a monetary wallet. A `ServicePriceVersion` defines cash currency/minor amount, integer unit grant, model/service rate schedule, rounding and tax/terms version. A purchased lot stores original provider charge/invoice/line, BillingOrganization, currency, unit grant, adjustments, consumed/reserved/frozen/available units and policy version. Do not present units as deposits, interest-bearing funds, tradable tokens or money redeemable on demand. Record a purchaser refund as reversal of an original service sale. Processor approval remains necessary; naming it a credit does not settle its legal classification.

`CheckoutIntent`, `SubscriptionProjection`, `ServiceEntitlement`, `SeatAssignment`, `CreditLot`, `UsageReservation`, `UsageReceipt`, `RefundCase` and `ReconciliationCursor` are validated proposed contracts in WP35, not new implemented services. Cash records and consumption entries have different units. Corrections are append-only and reference their originals. Uniqueness includes provider, account, environment, purchase/invoice/line and operation/attempt; event ID dedupe alone cannot prevent two different events from granting one purchase twice. An invoice credit note, refund and canceled subscription are not interchangeable. Included allowances, if later offered, require a unique subscription-period lot and an explicit expiry/rollover policy before launch.

For a lot, `available = granted + unit corrections - consumed - reserved - refund/dispute frozen`, subject to lot policy and nonnegative spendability. Atomic transactions prevent two concurrent spends or refund/spend races. Stable job/attempt keys prevent duplicate settlement across parents/children, callbacks and retries. Unknown external usage keeps a bounded reservation, with age alert and reconciliation owner; never release just because a worker lease timed out while provider work may continue. Never bill an invented usage amount. Price snapshots and verified actuals explain any discrepancy without silent excess debit beyond the authorized ceiling.

Use separate signing secrets, ingress namespaces and account/environment bindings for Studio billing and creator events. Verify raw bytes and signature with the selected provider's official method; enforce signed delivery timestamp tolerance where provided. A timestamp inside unsigned JSON is not replay protection. Persist a durable inbox before acknowledgement, then process idempotently through transactional/outbox operations. Stripe/Paddle event IDs and provider object versions differ from Lemon Squeezy's body HMAC: do not invent a common signed timestamp or event ID. For providers lacking a unique event ID, persist canonical payload digest plus object/event/version keys and reconcile authenticated history; duplicated effects remain impossible. Rotate secrets with a bounded overlap and keep dedupe/reversal tombstones through documented replay/restore windows.

Late valid events are not automatically invalid business data. An old capture with an expired delivery signature is rejected; provider redelivery with a valid new signature can still describe an old object. Deduplicate it and resolve current authoritative state rather than order by delivery time. Refund-before-payment, dispute-after-consumption and cancellation/renewal races must never resurrect a grant. Fetch current provider object/history under the bound account, serialize per aggregate and use version/causal guards; if evidence is insufficient, hold access/new spend as pending and reconcile. A restored database stays fenced until provider purchases, refunds, subscriptions, reservations and cursors are reconciled; rebuilding projections cannot regrant a purchase.

Chargebacks can exceed unused units after consumed work. Freeze remaining affected units, cancel queued work, stop new paid dispatch under the reviewed risk policy and record financial loss/receivable separately from a nonnegative service-unit balance. Do not fabricate available credit, auto-charge another card, confiscate unrelated merchant earnings or trigger bank recovery. Refund/dispute reversal creates a once-only correction after provider confirmation. Provider-processing reserves/holds and our operational cash reserve for refunds/chargebacks are different from job unit reservations.

## Creator selling and getting paid

1. **Select seller and destination.** In the offer, choose legal entity, MerchantConnection and test/live mode; show who sells, who processes or is MoR for which duties, who handles tax/refunds/support and where buyers receive files/access. No Studio paid plan or usage pack is required solely for a creator-owned external checkout. Provider account access, official API entitlement and any always-on event/delivery service are separate costs and gates.
2. **Complete eligibility outside Studio.** Provider-hosted onboarding performs relevant KYC/business/bank/tax verification. Seller country, payout bank country/currency, supported product type and buyer countries are separate checks. Product approval covers actual AI-generated content, rights, claims, subscriptions/coaching/services and any media/marketing features. Imported merchant IDs are verified through that authorized account. Never collect passwords/bank documents in prompts or assume a partner's merchant approval covers the operator.
3. **Prepare an original offer.** Bind artifact hash/version, price/currency/tax behavior, license/terms, refund policy, buyer support and delivery method. For Lemon Squeezy, prepare an export plus setup checklist, require manual provider product/file upload if needed, then read back product/variant/file IDs and record an observed mapping. A manual action is labeled manual; Checkout creation is not a product-upload API. Missing scopes prevent automation. Free/discount/trial/recurring installments activate only with provider-specific acceptance.
4. **Test then publish.** Separate provider sandbox from production credentials, accounts, products and webhook secrets. Synthetic/test purchases grant only test access. Verify a test buyer can retrieve the correct version and another buyer cannot. Preview exact merchant and listing; a project/account switch invalidates stale action approval. A live checkout/publication needs a specific user action after E2/E3/E6 acceptance, not an agent's pricing recommendation.
5. **Buyer checkout and access.** Buyers use provider checkout and disclosed identity/access method, then see pending/paid/failed plus provider invoice/receipt and support. Verified paid status leads to fulfillment once, including delayed payments and interrupted redirects. Provider-managed delivery must work with the desktop closed. Download links are scoped and expire as appropriate; course/membership enrollment, renewal, cancellation and revocation are independent capabilities. A subscription is not automatically a completed course and files already downloaded cannot be remotely erased.
6. **Seller sees status, not promised earnings.** Show gross, discounts, tax, fees, refunds/disputes, net, provider pending/available/held/reserved balances, payout currency/FX and reported payout status with observed time/completeness. An order, invoice, estimated net, provider transfer and bank payout are different objects. Mark missing data unknown. Scheduled/in-transit/failed/returned/provider-reported-paid statuses remain distinct; do not claim confirmed bank receipt without evidence. Hosted provider dashboard handoff handles bank changes and withdrawals; Studio has no cash-out button backed by its usage ledger. At M4C offer a verified dashboard/status handoff; full API reconciliation/admin is M7D, analytics M4D.
7. **Refund and reconcile.** At M4C link the authorized seller to provider refund/dispute support and ingest the result; WP47 later adds approved scoped initiation and partial-refund/plan administration. Bind amount, remaining refundable ceiling, currency, original payment, merchant, policy and actor. Recheck at execution. Reconcile refund, transfer reversal, fees, tax credit note and buyer access separately; returning money does not inherently reverse a partner transfer or cancel a subscription. No double refund during a chargeback. Preserve evidence deadlines, provider-imposed actions and dispute fees. Revocation or expired credentials pause new merchant actions and disclose stale projections without breaking provider-held buyer delivery.

For Stripe Connect, prefer a creator-direct charge model for single-seller software if later approved: configure hosted onboarding/dashboard and explicitly choose fee-payer and negative-balance/loss responsibility. Direct-charge refunds/disputes affect the connected account's balance, but liability depends on the actual configuration/agreement; never claim we have no risk. Destination charges and separate charges/transfers can debit the platform and require additional reserves, country/region/funds-flow eligibility and explicit transfer reversal logic. They stay disabled initially. A provider transfer is not a bank payout, an affiliate feature is not a general joint-venture split, and cross-border support is not universal. Existing WP20 split scenarios remain assumptions, not payment instructions. No holding buyer funds ourselves, self-issued money, pooled seller balances, escrow or unlicensed payout engine.

## Operations, privacy and economics

WP37 supplies a named finance/support/on-call owner, provider reconciliation schedule/cursors, queue lag/dead-letter and signature alerts, rate/velocity/concurrency caps, spend limits, abuse/cost-loss reserve and billing incident runbooks. Compare processor settled cash, invoice/charge/refund/dispute records, purchased lots and worker usage independently; unknown fees or payouts are not silently zero. Reconciliation cadence, reserve amount, retention periods and response targets must be signed off against measured pilot volume before live activation. No generic install, auth, cloud vendor or connector broker provides these operations by itself.

Minimize billing/customer data; never log PAN/CVC, tokens, full portal URLs or raw identity documents. Store limited receipt references, legal tax fields and audit metadata under role/tenant access with encrypted service custody and retention controls. Keep support exports and signed download links scoped. Provider subprocessors, international destinations, data rights and deletion constraints are disclosed. Cancellation, loss of a seat, organization deletion and processor disconnection are different actions. A user deletion tombstone must survive backup restoration without discarding records required for accounting, disputes or once-only refunds. Retention is jurisdiction/contract-specific; no universal erasure or compliance guarantee.

The proposed USD 19.95 service pack is a unit-economics experiment, not a monthly subscription price, fixed product count or unlimited usage promise. Choose grant/rates only after measuring representative successes, retries, failed/unknown jobs, premium/media costs, rendering/storage/egress, payment/Billing/tax/MoR fees, FX, support, refunds/disputes/fraud, taxes excluded from revenue and operational overhead. Separate taxes, revenue, deferred service obligation and provider spend. Proposed metric: contribution = cash excluding collected transaction tax - payment/MoR/FX costs - actual service/infrastructure delivery cost - support/refund/dispute allowance. Track unconsumed purchased-unit obligations and reserve cash independently; do not treat the entire pack price as earned profit. Publish rates, quote limits and material price changes; offer local/manual routes when hosting does not qualify economically.

## Integrated activation and data-exit scope

SERVICES.md separates E4 pre-build rights/provider/terms/budget decisions from public-paid activation evidence produced by WP37. Component payment/worker fixtures and separately authorized capped non-public pilots do not imply public checkout readiness. The existing commercial/economic and independent finance/security gates remain required.

The proposed 30-day authenticated read/export window includes latest retained cloud revisions/assets without funded AI generation or a renewal purchase. WP34 verifies snapshots and external-operation custody before fenced cloud-to-local handback. Compute exhaustion, service lapse, revoked membership, expired login and outage have different results; grace never bypasses authorization or promises unavailable remote bytes. Old local writers stay fenced; deliberate offline copies get new identities with no transferred financial/action authority.

WP27/30 first commerce activation owns the paid-but-not-delivered support/ingress/shutdown route even before hosted WP37. Current deletion/revocation state outside a rewound backup governs managed restore; a historical archive without it cannot reactivate finance grants or deleted-content retrieval. Cost models include named payers for retained assets/export grace and other fixed/idle obligations at zero new top-ups, preserving the existing unit/cash separation and reversal policy.
