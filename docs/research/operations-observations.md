# Lead, campaign and CRM workflow observations

Research date: 2026-11-12. Complements the [product demo observations](demo-observations.md). Official tutorial recordings, documentation bodies and source images were inspected. No account, live lead-search API, domain purchase, campaign publication or email send was used. Older recordings establish historical interface behavior, not today's authenticated service availability.

## Discovery and lead management

An [official ListKit tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PNpGaGdRNug&t=50s) shows inclusion/exclusion filters and job-title selection. Its [current search guide](https://docs.listkit.io/en/articles/13263617-how-do-i-use-search-to-build-a-lead-list) describes company/person criteria, saved searches, new-versus-exported matches, bounded export quantity, per-company limits, duplicate exclusion and order history.

Our requirement is a source-aware prospect workspace with saved queries, permitted data connectors/imports, deduplication, provenance and portable export. Reproducing a search interface does not supply ListKit's proprietary contact database. Data access, contact validity, provider credits and reuse rights remain connector-specific. Public profile evidence is not automatically verified contact information or consent to outreach.

## Campaign composition and sender setup

The [official campaign guide](https://docs.listkit.io/en/articles/13789882-how-do-i-create-a-campaign-in-email-engine) provides annotated interface images. The workflow covers campaign identity and recipients, sequence composition, sender/schedule configuration, and final review. Images show AI assistance, variants, delayed follow-ups, selectable sending accounts and editable review sections. Publishing can start scheduled delivery; it is an external action, not a harmless preview.

A separate [sender-setup recording](https://listkit.wistia.com/medias/hceot8g486) shows domain suggestions/availability and assignment of sender details to domains. These are recorded controls, not purchases or deliverability tests performed here.

Our campaign builder must connect recipients, versioned variants/follow-ups, sender identities, timezones/windows, limits, suppression state and an exact reviewable execution plan. Domain/mailbox readiness is a connection prerequisite. Availability checking cannot authorize a purchase; sender identities must be authorized. Automatic domain acquisition or unlimited deliverability is not implied.

## CRM and collaboration

The [official Flozy board tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BqD-bCDvzbE&t=80s) shows a contact/deal card with status, value, tags, due date, assignee and attachments. Later frames show pipeline columns and an export-completion message. Captions discuss private/client-linked boards and import/export. No exported file was independently checked. A separate official homepage montage is stylized promotional UI, not an operational test.

Our contact workspace therefore includes configurable boards, structured cards, relationship history, ownership/assignment, tasks and safe import/export. Views share the same contact/project records rather than duplicating them. Client-linked visibility requires explicit scoped access; it must not follow merely from attaching a card to a board.

## Partnership audit assets and attribution limits

The [public outreach-audit training](https://www.monetise.com/resources/shs-vault/mini-vault/the-ghostwriter-os-outreach-loom-audit) contains recorded examples of explaining a creator/product proposal through a short walkthrough and reviewing it. This supports an original partner-audit brief and optional walkthrough asset with evidence, assumptions and next steps. Displayed revenue estimates are hypotheses, not sales evidence.

This track did not capture Ghostwriter's URL-to-generated-result execution. The separate older walkthrough in demo-observations.md remains evidence of its brief/context-to-response flow. One secondary image is visibly branded Synthesise and is excluded as Ghostwriter evidence. No private prompt catalog, complete current agent census, live CRM account or independent sending reliability was established.

Raw frames, 22 campaign documentation images, source metadata and hashes remain in the private research workspace. They are reference material, not bundled application assets.
