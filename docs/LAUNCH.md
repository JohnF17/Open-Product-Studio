# Business model and launch plan

Status: proposals and validation criteria, not revenue forecasts. No payment or donation account has been connected.

## Positioning

“Build a useful digital product with AI you choose. Keep your files. See what it costs.”

Lead with a visible result and honest process. The main audience is people with knowledge or a practical customer problem who need help turning it into a clear, usable asset. A secondary audience is technical users who already pay for an AI CLI and want a guided workflow outside coding.

Originality and reputation are assets. Do not use a competitor's name, face, workbook designs, proprietary prompts, testimonials, or unsupported misconduct claims in launch material. Study public workflows and build original ones. No wording can guarantee freedom from a takedown; provenance records and accurate, original work are better protection than clever insinuation.

## Free and paid boundaries

**Free local edition:** complete first workflow, all local exports, provider/model selection, local data ownership, original tutorials, and no mandatory account. Users fund their chosen provider or hardware. Avoid making useful free features disappear after adoption.

**Optional cloud:** M5A accounts/explicit transfer, M5B hosted execution/credits and M6C collaborative content editing have separate gates in the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md). Separate recurring service/storage/seat subscriptions from one-time nontransferable prepaid inference/service units, using the [payment design](PAYMENTS.md). Local access and creator-owned external checkout require neither. A USD 19.95 service pack is a denomination to test, not a promised subscription price, month of use, fixed product count, cash balance or unlimited premium models. Paid seats cannot activate unshipped collaboration.

At checkout show the payer organization and seller/MoR, test/live mode, currency/total/taxes, recurring interval/seats or exact service-unit grant/rate conversion, terms/refund/expiry version and invoice/receipt/support route. Stripe Checkout/Billing/portal is the conditional Studio reference under E4; ordinary processing leaves our merchant tax/refund/dispute duties explicit. Paddle needs a separate full-offering approval/MoR decision, particularly for media/marketing/phone functions. No automatic processor switch. Select the actual legal entity/country from owner-provided evidence, never machine locale. Only verified server payment state enables access; a return screen is not payment evidence.

Do not offer raw pooled consumer-subscription access. Local subscriptions remain each user's own connection under supported provider terms. Use official commercial API agreements for a hosted lane. Consumer account limits and free tiers are not the platform's inventory to resell.

## Creator commerce and payment operations

Propose creator-owned approved Lemon Squeezy stores for original downloads, provider checkout/delivery and manual product/file setup where API writes are unavailable. Do not sell all creators' goods through our store. Whop/course/membership needs separate capability/account approval; professional services may require a different invoice provider. Creator onboarding verifies legal entity, seller/payout countries and currencies, actual product/AI rights and tax/refund/support duties. MoR branding does not promise all taxes, no disputes or instant earnings. Connect/splits stay disabled pending reviewed funds flow and liability. See the [official decisions](plans/2026-11-13-payment-provider-decisions.md).

Before collecting money, publish checkout/cancellation/refund/retention/support terms, name a finance/on-call owner, accept country/entity/product and commercial API rights, test payment/renewal/delayed/refund/dispute/revocation and restore races, reconcile cash/ledger/usage and pilot measured limits. Offer authenticated export/read grace after service lapse (proposed 30 days); no renewal payment to retrieve owned work. Delete profiles/content with disclosed finance/dispute/backup exceptions and provider-held work inventory. Optional verified donations stay separate.

## Unit economics before pricing

Instrument measured input/output tokens, tools, rendering, storage, egress, retries and payment costs. Use representative workflows including failures and large documents. Let `P` be cash collected excluding transaction tax. Measure contribution as `P - payment/MoR/Billing/tax-service/FX costs - actual service and infrastructure delivery cost - support/refund/dispute/abuse allowance`. Track unconsumed purchased-service obligations and cash-loss reserves separately; neither an unused grant nor the whole collected pack price is earned profit. Include failures, retries and pending usage. A positive contribution does not cover all development/operational overhead.

Choose exact integer service units with a public price/rate conversion and immutable job quotes, separate from cash currency records. This replaces the earlier currency-wallet proposal and permits only consumption of our named services, not third-party payments, transfers or cash withdrawal. Proposed first packs have no auto-recharge/overage and no expiry of unused purchased units; E4 must approve refund/mandatory-rights, promotional expiry and tax treatment. Storage/service/team subscriptions are priced separately only when they deliver accepted value. Cancellation preserves packs and local files; a funded balance cannot authorize an unbounded job.

No recommendation to buy ads yet. Acquisition costs and conversion rates are unknown. Test willingness to use, usefulness of exports, and support effort before optimizing a paid funnel.

## Validate the product before scaling

Recruit five varied pilot users privately with permission: at least two who are not technical, one existing CLI user, one subject expert, and one creator/service-business user. Give them a real task and watch where they get stuck. Record consent and avoid recording private client data.

Pass local usability when four of five can complete/export without intervention, no critical data is lost, all can explain where their data went and what they paid, and the products pass an explicit usefulness review. Ask intended readers to try the outputs; founder enthusiasm is not customer validation. Repeat after fixes with fresh users where feasible.

## Advertising sequence

1. **Show the work:** publish one complete screen recording creating a small useful product, including the source check, editing, limitations, engine selection and export. Caption it. Provide the example files under a clear license.
2. **Explain ownership:** demonstrate switching providers, failed quota handling, offline editing, and portable files. Show the real operation, with secrets redacted.
3. **Reach relevant communities:** post a concise build story in open-source, creator, and small-business communities that allow it. Read each community's rules and disclose authorship. Do not mass-message critics of another seller or astroturf support.
4. **Publish useful comparisons:** compare visible features, setup burden, privacy boundaries and total costs with dated sources. No fabricated benchmarks, earnings screenshots, urgency counters or testimonial generators.
5. **Launch the repository:** good README, actual quickstart, signed/verified release instructions, example project, limitations, roadmap, beginner issues, contribution and security policies.
6. **Launch cloud only after validation:** publish pricing and a cost calculator using measured rates. Test a small paid cohort before paid acquisition.

Example launch copy, to use only when the corresponding capabilities ship:

> I built an open-source workspace for turning a practical idea into a guide, workbook, and launch kit. It connects to your supported AI CLI, an official API, or a local model. You choose the model, review the sources, and keep the exported files. Here's a complete example, including the edits it needed.

README humor should stay brief and general. “A progress bar should measure work, not manufacture urgency” communicates a value without building the brand around a personal dispute. The user's frustration is a reason to make transparent software; it should not be the entire product identity.

## Sustainable support

After maintainers choose and verify URLs, add GitHub Sponsors and optionally Buy Me a Coffee or another supported donation service to `.github/FUNDING.yml` and the README. Confirm payout eligibility and fees first. Do not create fictional links, open accounts, make purchases, or ask for funds on behalf of the owner.

Publish what funding pays for: audits, hosting, accessibility work, maintainers, and support. Keep donations voluntary. Sponsors do not obtain privileged access to user projects, secret roadmap influence, or automatic merge rights. Consider paid support/setup and team convenience after actual demand; keep local product ownership intact.

## Content and product-quality policy

Differentiate with examples, working templates, correct calculations, tested tools, clear permissions, and maintenance. Require subject review for high-stakes content. Do not encourage people without expertise to sell unreviewed health, financial or legal guidance. Market the tool as assistance with making and evaluating a product, never an automatic income machine.

Source retrieval is not permission to repackage someone else's work. Record licensing/ownership/permission for imported material and block public redistribution where permission is unknown. Keep private research notes outside the public repository.

## Decisions to revisit with evidence

Choose a cleared brand/domain before public marketing. Choose the merchant country and processor before billing work. Set concrete hosted retention and refund terms before checkout. Decide whether demand justifies full collaboration or more product formats after the first workflow has repeat users. A local-first architecture is the proposed first implementation, not a claim that nontechnical customers will prefer installing software.

## Service readiness and complete product demonstration

Use [SERVICES.md](SERVICES.md) for the minimal topology and payer/cost/exit register. Demonstrate business-project creation/import, guided first export, safe project switching, late-job recovery and a direct-product launch without compulsory creator/email setup. [PROJECT-EXPERIENCE.md](PROJECT-EXPERIENCE.md) supplies accessibility, motion and external-reader acceptance. Retain all wider modules with truthful gates.

Price idle obligations explicitly: retained content/backups/export grace, ingress, browser/worker uptime and mailbox/number/domain renewals each need a named payer and bounded policy even with zero new top-ups. Low-use, normal-use, high-retry and abusive-ingress cohorts must pass WP37's measured gate. First commerce/mail/phone activation also needs provider-native shutdown/support and a credential-free outage runbook; it cannot wait for a later hosted or standing-action gate.

E4 pre-build provider/rights/terms and bounded pilot decisions are distinct from public-paid activation evidence. No price or hosted availability claim until WP37 economics/operations and actual account/contract approval. Authenticated retained-content export/handback after lapse requires no purchase; finalized duration/capacity/deadline, reauthentication, revoked access and outage behavior must be disclosed before checkout. Local exports and provider-held buyer delivery remain independent.

Before a guided Windows CLI claim, publish a build-specific compatibility entry for at least one qualified route. Before advertising a complete digital product workflow, record the first-result pilot, export/reopen and recovery witnesses in the [execution and release decisions](plans/2026-11-18-execution-and-release-decisions.md).
